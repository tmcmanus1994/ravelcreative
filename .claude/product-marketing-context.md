# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-02-03*

## Product Overview

**One-liner:** Ravel Creative partners with businesses to execute on all of their video content needs.

**What it does:** Full-service video content agency that handles everything from scripting and filming to editing, ad copy, posting schedules, and ad management. We don't just deliver videos — we ensure businesses get maximum value from every piece of content.

**Product category:** Video content agency / Video marketing partner

**Product type:** Service (B2B)

**Business model:** Monthly retainer packages (3, 6, or 12 month terms)

## Services

- Video Scripts
- Filming (on-site)
- Video Editing
- Ad Copy
- Posting Schedule Strategy
- Ad Management/Service

## Target Audience

**Target companies:**

- Local businesses (geographic focus for on-site work)

**Decision-makers:** Business Owner or Marketing Manager

**Primary use case:** Businesses that need consistent, high-quality video content but don't have the time, expertise, or team to execute an effective video strategy.

**Jobs to be done:**

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** Businesses don't know how to use video effectively in the attention economy.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Freelancers and agencies deliver one-off videos with limited ongoing value
- Media shelf life is painfully short — a single video isn't enough
- No guidance on how to maximize content across platforms and time

**What it costs them:**
- Wasted budget on videos that get posted once and forgotten
- Missed opportunities to connect with their audience
- Falling behind competitors who show up consistently

**Emotional tension:**
- Overwhelmed by the content demands of modern marketing
- Frustrated by past video investments that didn't deliver results
- Anxious about being invisible while competitors dominate feeds

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct competitors:**
- Freelance videographers — Fall short because they deliver files, not strategy. One video, limited value.
- Local video production agencies — Same issue: project-based work without ongoing content execution.

**Secondary competitors:**
- In-house marketing teams — Often lack video expertise or bandwidth for consistent output.
- DIY tools (Canva, CapCut, etc.) — Quality and consistency suffer; still requires significant time investment.

**Indirect competitors:**
- Doing nothing / staying with photos and text — Increasingly ineffective as video dominates attention.

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**
- End-to-end execution, not just deliverables
- Retainer model ensures consistency (not one-and-done)
- Strategy + production + distribution in one partner
- AI-augmented editing for speed without sacrificing quality

**How we do it differently:** Instead of handing over a video file and walking away, we guarantee the best use of your video content — from creation through posting and optimization.

**Why that's better:** Clients get consistent presence, maximized ROI on every shoot, and a true partner invested in their results over months, not a single project.

**Why customers choose us:**
- Don't have time to manage video themselves
- Want consistency without hiring in-house
- Previous one-off videos didn't move the needle
- Need a partner who understands both production AND strategy
