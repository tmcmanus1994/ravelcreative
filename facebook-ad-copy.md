## Quick Reference: Copy Elements

### Headlines That Work
- Problem-focused: "Your Video Is Collecting Dust"
- Transformation-focused: "90 Days Will Change Everything"
- Curiosity-focused: "Why Your Content Isn't Working"
- Comparison-focused: "Your Competitor Posts 3x a Week"

### Primary Text Structure
1. Hook (pain point or bold statement)
2. Before/After or Problem/Solution
3. What Ravel Creative does
4. Local focus mention
5. CTA

### Descriptions (Keep Short)
- Include the offer: "Free discovery call"
- Reinforce the value: "Full-service video content"
- Add credibility: "10 years experience"

### Words to Use
- Partner (not vendor)
- Consistent / Consistency
- Strategy / Strategic
- Full-service
- Monthly retainer
- Central Arkansas / Local

### Words to Avoid
- Viral
- Cheap / Affordable
- Quick turnaround
- Guaranteed results (be careful with claims)

## Testing Recommendations

**Test in pairs:**
- Headline A vs. Headline B (same primary text)
- Pain-focused vs. Aspiration-focused primary text
- Short description vs. CTA-heavy description

**Start with:**
- Before/After #2 (One Video Problem) — addresses common objection
- Founder VSL #1 (Hard Truth) — educational hook

**Monitor:**
- CTR (Click-through rate) — headline effectiveness
- Video watch time — hook effectiveness
- Conversion rate — offer/CTA effectiveness
