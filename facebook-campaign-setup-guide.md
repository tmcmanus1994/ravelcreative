# Facebook Lead Generation Campaign Setup Guide

## Campaign Overview

**Objective:** Lead Generation (build email list)
**Offer:** Free Video Content Audit
**A/B Test:** Founder VSL (Video) vs. Before/After (Static Image)
**Target:** Central Arkansas business owners and marketing managers

---

## Step 1: Campaign Level Settings

### In Ads Manager, Click "Create"

**Campaign Objective:** Leads

**Campaign Name:** `Ravel Creative - Lead Gen - Content Audit - Feb 2026`

### A/B Test Setup
- Toggle ON: "A/B Test"
- Variable: **Creative** (this lets you test video vs. image)
- Facebook will automatically split traffic 50/50

### Campaign Budget
**Budget Type:** Campaign Budget Optimization (CBO) — ON

**Daily Budget:** $20-50/day to start
- $20/day = ~$600/month (conservative start)
- $50/day = ~$1,500/month (faster data)

**Recommendation:** Start at $30/day. This gives you enough data without burning budget while learning.

### Bid Strategy
**Select:** Lowest cost (default)
- Let Facebook optimize for you initially
- Switch to cost cap later once you know your target cost per lead

---

## Step 2: Ad Set Level Settings

You'll have 2 ad sets (one for each creative in the A/B test). Settings should be IDENTICAL except for the creative.

### Ad Set Names
- Ad Set A: `VSL Video - Central AR - Business Owners`

### Conversion Location
**Select:** Instant Forms

## Step 4: Lead Form Setup

### Create Instant Form

**Form Name:** `Content Audit Lead Form - Feb 2026`

**Form Type:** More Volume
- Simpler form, higher conversion rate
- Use "Higher Intent" later if lead quality is low

---

### Form Sections

#### 1. Intro (Optional but recommended)
**Headline:** Claim Your Free Video Content Audit
**Image:** Your logo or a professional photo of you
**Description:** In 20 minutes, I'll review your current content strategy and show you exactly what's working, what isn't, and how to fix it. No pitch, no pressure.

---

#### 2. Questions

**Pre-filled fields (pulled from Facebook):**
- Full Name
- Email
- Phone Number

**Custom Questions (add 1-2 to qualify leads):**

Question 1:
- Type: Multiple Choice
- Question: "What best describes your business?"
- Options:
  - Small business / Local service
  - Tech startup
  - Law firm
  - Medical or dental practice
  - Other professional service
  - Other

Question 2 (Optional):
- Type: Multiple Choice
- Question: "Are you currently investing in video content?"
- Options:
  - Yes, but it's not working well
  - Sometimes, but not consistently
  - No, but I want to start
  - No, not interested (screen out)

---

#### 3. Privacy Policy
**Link:** Add your website privacy policy URL
- If you don't have one, create a simple page or use a generator
- Required by Facebook

## Step 6: Post-Launch Monitoring

### First 48 Hours
- Check that ads are approved and delivering
- Don't make changes yet — let it learn

### Days 3-7
Check these metrics daily:

| Metric | Target | If Below Target |
|--------|--------|-----------------|
| CTR (Click-through rate) | >1% | Test new headlines/creative |
| CPM (Cost per 1,000 impressions) | <$15 | Audience may be too narrow |
| Cost per Lead | <$15-25 | Adjust audience or creative |
| Form completion rate | >30% | Simplify form |

### Day 7-14
- Review A/B test results
- Declare winner (needs 95% confidence)
- Kill losing ad, scale winning ad

## Budget Recommendations

### Conservative Start
| Daily Budget | Monthly Spend | Expected Leads (at $15/lead) |
|--------------|---------------|------------------------------|
| $20/day | ~$600 | 40 leads |
| $30/day | ~$900 | 60 leads |
| $50/day | ~$1,500 | 100 leads |

### Scaling Plan
1. **Week 1-2:** $30/day, run A/B test
2. **Week 3:** Winner declared, increase to $50/day
3. **Week 4+:** If CPL stays good, scale to $75-100/day

---

## Lead Follow-Up (Critical!)

Leads are worthless if you don't follow up fast.

### Response Time
- **Goal:** Respond within 5 minutes
- **Minimum:** Same business day
- **Data:** Leads contacted within 5 min are 9x more likely to convert

### Recommended Flow
1. **Instant:** Automated email confirming their request
2. **Within 1 hour:** Personal email or call to schedule audit
3. **Day 2:** Follow-up if no response
4. **Day 4:** Second follow-up
5. **Day 7:** Final follow-up, then move to nurture sequence

### Connect Lead Form to CRM
Options:
- **Zapier:** Connect Facebook Lead Ads → Email/CRM
- **Meta Business Suite:** Manual download (not ideal)
- **CRM native integration:** HubSpot, GoHighLevel, etc.
