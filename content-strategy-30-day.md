# 30-Day Content Strategy: Ravel Creative

**Goal:** Build brand awareness in Central Arkansas and generate leads for video content retainer services

**Platforms:** Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn

**Target Audience:** Business owners and marketing managers at small businesses, tech startups, law firms, and private practices in Central Arkansas

---

## Content Pillars

Build every post around these 5 pillars:

| Pillar | % | What It Covers |
|--------|---|----------------|
| **Educational** | 30% | Video tips, attention economy insights, content strategy advice |
| **Behind-the-Scenes** | 25% | Your process, editing workflow, on-set moments, equipment |
| **Local Business Love** | 20% | Spotlight Central AR businesses, local success stories, community |
| **Results & Proof** | 15% | Case studies, before/after, metrics, client wins |
| **Personal/Story** | 10% | Your journey, why you started, lessons learned |

---

## Platform Strategy

### LinkedIn
**Frequency:** 4x per week (Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri)
**Best for:** Reaching business owners and marketing managers
**Focus:** Educational content, thought leadership, business case for video

**Format mix:**
- Text posts with hooks (60%)
- Carousels/documents (30%)
- Video clips (10%)

**Key tactics:**
- No links in post body (put in comments)
- First line = hook that stops the scroll
- Ask questions to drive comments
- Engage with local business leaders' posts daily

---

### Facebook
**Frequency:** 5x per week
**Best for:** Local community, established business owners
**Focus:** Community connection, local spotlight, native video

**Format mix:**
- Native video (40%)
- Photo + story posts (30%)
- Local business tags/shoutouts (20%)
- Live video (10% - weekly)

**Key tactics:**
- Join and engage in Central Arkansas business groups
- Tag local businesses when relevant
- Use location tags (Little Rock, Conway, etc.)
- Facebook Lives build trust fast

---

### Instagram
**Frequency:** 5x per week feed + daily Stories
**Best for:** Showcasing visual work, reaching younger business owners
**Focus:** Behind-the-scenes, Reels, portfolio showcase

**Format mix:**
- Reels (50%) - prioritized by algorithm
- Carousels (30%)
- Single image + caption (10%)
- Stories (daily)

**Key tactics:**
- Reels get 2x reach - prioritize short video tips
- Use local hashtags (#CentralArkansas #LittleRockBusiness #ArkansasSmallBusiness)
- Behind-the-scenes Stories of shoots
- Save highlights: Services, Process, Work, Testimonials

## Content Creation Batching

### Weekly Time Block (2-3 hours)

**Day 1: Planning (30 min)**
- Review last week's performance
- Outline this week's posts
- Note any local events/news to reference

**Day 2: Creation (90 min)**
- Write 4 LinkedIn posts
- Create 2 carousels
- Script 3 Reels
- Draft Facebook posts

**Day 3: Filming (30-60 min)**
- Batch film 3-5 Reels
- Capture BTS content for Stories
- Record any native Facebook videos

**Ongoing: Schedule**
- Use scheduling tool (Later, Buffer, or native)
- Leave room for spontaneous/reactive posts
